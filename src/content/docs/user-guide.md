# User guide

Task-oriented flows for the web app. Prefer the companion [Visual guides](/docs/visual-guides) page for diagrams and a screen map.

Adjust labels if your deployment differs slightly.

## Use AI chat safely

1. Open [/chat](/chat).
2. Ask specific questions; include gestational week or key symptoms when relevant.
3. Prefer **short threads** for urgent concerns and escalate to a clinician.
4. For **voice-style** answers, use voice mode when the UI exposes it (shorter, spoken phrasing).

Remember: AI can be wrong; always verify with a professional for diagnosis or treatment.

## Log vitals

1. Go to [/vitals](/vitals).
2. Add a new entry with the measurements you have.
3. Review history over time with your care team at visits.

## Run a symptom check

1. Open [/symptoms](/symptoms).
2. Complete the flow honestly.
3. Read the result as **educational triage**, not a diagnosis. Seek urgent care when the app or your instinct says so.

## Plan your day

1. Visit [/planner](/planner).
2. Review or fill **daily** planner entries.
3. Use food suggestions when shown to diversify nutrition within your cultural context.

## Participate in community

1. Browse [/community](/community).
2. Use **Create** to author a post; keep it supportive and on-topic.
3. **Like** and **comment** respectfully; use **Report** for concerning content.
4. Open a **member profile** from a post or comment avatar when available.

## Manage notifications

1. Use the **bell** in the app header for a quick sheet, or open [/notifications](/notifications).
2. Mark items read as you go; some builds mark all read when opening the sheet.

## Upload a medical report

1. Open [/reports](/reports).
2. Follow upload or capture steps.
3. Read the summary alongside your clinician; do not self-treat from AI text alone.

## Change settings

- [/settings](/settings) — account and app preferences.
- [/help](/help) — support email when `NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPPORT_EMAIL` is configured.

## Emergency

Use [/emergency](/emergency) for hotline quick links. **Not** a substitute for calling emergency services.
