# About MaaCare

MaaCare is an AI-assisted maternal health companion. It helps people track pregnancy and postpartum journeys, log vitals and symptoms, understand medical reports, find nearby care, connect with community support, and chat with a bilingual assistant grounded in curated health knowledge and personal profile data.

**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Languages:** English and Bangla (বাংলা)

## The problem

Many expectant and new parents do not have continuous, understandable health guidance between clinical visits. Medical documents are often hard to read. Emergency resources can be difficult to find quickly. Generic apps rarely combine pregnancy context, bilingual support, community, and AI explanation in one place.

MaaCare focuses on making everyday maternal health support more accessible, especially for users who prefer Bangla or a mix of English and Bangla in daily conversation.

## Who it is for

**Pregnant individuals** tracking their own journey  
**Parents and caregivers** supporting someone else's pregnancy or postpartum period  
**Clinicians and students** who use the app with a professional or learning profile  
**Community members** who want peer support through posts and messages  
**Operators** with an admin role who moderate content and manage the knowledge base

## What makes it different

Personalized guidance uses your profile, pregnancy stage, and logged health data when you choose to record it. The assistant can reply in English or Bangla and search a curated knowledge base before answering. Emergency information and nearby facilities are built into the product. Community features include moderation and reporting tools.

## Important disclaimer

MaaCare is **not** a substitute for a qualified clinician. AI answers are for education and support only. They are not a diagnosis. For emergencies, use local emergency services and the in-app **Emergency** area.
