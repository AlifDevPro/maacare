# Features

The sections below summarize major product areas. A **feature directory** table is rendered under this page in the app with direct links into the product.

## AI & guidance

- **Chat** — conversational help with optional voice-style replies; uses retrieval-augmented knowledge and safe escalation language.
- **Guidance topics** — short educational pages (e.g. hydration, movement) under `/guidance/...`.

## Health tracking

- **Vitals** — log blood pressure, weight, and related readings.
- **Symptoms** — structured checklists with severity-oriented messaging (educational only).
- **Planner** — day-level hydration and nutrition-style planning.
- **Appointments** — simple visit tracking.

## Community

- **Feed** — browse and search posts.
- **Create post** — rich text and optional images.
- **Threaded comments** — reply chains with moderation hooks.
- **Member profiles** — community-safe cards; optional extended pregnancy summary when the member allows it.

## Safety & locality

- **Emergency** — hotlines and safety copy (also reachable without an account where configured).
- **Facilities** — nearby discovery when location is available.

## Reports

- **Report simplifier** — upload or capture documents for plain-language summaries via server-side AI pipelines.

## Operations

- **Admin console** — users, knowledge corpus, community moderation, feedback triage (admin role only).
