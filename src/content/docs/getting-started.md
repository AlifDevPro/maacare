# Getting started

## Create an account

1. Open [Sign up](/signup) from the marketing site or documentation header.
2. Complete email verification if your project has OTP enabled ([Verify OTP](/verify-otp)).
3. After sign-in you land on the **app home** at [/app](/app).

## Sign in later

Use [/login](/login). Password reset flows live at [/forgot-password](/forgot-password) and [/reset-password](/reset-password).

## Language (English / Bangla)

- Profile and many screens respect your UI language.
- You can switch language from the **account menu** (signed-in header) or from [Settings](/settings) when available.
- The AI chat detects Bangla script and common **Banglish** patterns to reply in appropriate language when possible.

## Core navigation

- **Bottom navigation** (mobile): quick access to home, chat, community, and more depending on your build.
- **App header**: notifications, profile menu, and contextual back buttons on inner pages.

## Profile & pregnancy context

Open [/profile](/profile) to set display name, avatar, and pregnancy-related fields used to personalize guidance. Extended community visibility is **opt-in** where the product exposes it.

## Public vs signed-in pages

- **Public examples:** this documentation, [Emergency](/emergency) information.
- **Signed-in:** chat, vitals, community authoring, planner, appointments, reports analysis, and most APIs.
