# MaaCare documentation

Welcome to the **MaaCare** product and platform documentation. This hub is **readable without signing in**; the app itself still protects your data with Supabase Auth and row-level security.

## What MaaCare is

MaaCare is an AI-assisted maternal health companion: personalized pregnancy context, symptom and vitals logging, community support, and educational guidance in **English** and **বাংলা (Bangla)**.

## Important disclaimer

MaaCare is **not** a substitute for a qualified clinician. AI output is for education and support only. For emergencies, use local emergency services and the in-app **Emergency** area.
